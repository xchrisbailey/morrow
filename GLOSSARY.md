# Morrow

Morrow is a self-hosted personal hub for solo developers: notes, boards and todos in one place.

## Language

**Instance**:
One self-hosted installation of morrow, with its own data.
_Avoid_: Server, deployment, workspace

**Owner**:
The single person an Instance belongs to; the first and only account on it.
_Avoid_: Admin, user, account

**Scratchpad**:
A single free-form note that belongs to the Owner and lives on the home screen, outside any Space or Section.
_Avoid_: Quick note, inbox, draft
