Morrow design system

Foundations

The tokens and layouts behind Morrow, a personal hub for solo developers. Primitives hold raw values; semantic tokens name roles and change with the theme.

Color

Two themes, Day and Dusk. Warm sand paper and green-gray ink carry 90 to 95% of every screen. One muted sage accent (hue 155) marks the primary action, links, selection and focus. All values are OKLCH so steps are even. Components use the semantic roles below, never the ramps. Role names follow shadcn's (ADR-0003): primary is the sage accent, accent is the hover and selected wash.

Typography

Geist for everything you operate, Geist Serif (--font-display) for the brand, the greeting and page and section titles. 1.2 ratio from a 16px base; app UI sits at 14px and long-form text at 16px. Sizes from xl up are fluid between 360px and 1280px viewports. Nothing is smaller than 13px, and labels are sentence case at 13px medium, never tracked capitals.

Spacing

A 4px base with 2px half steps for control and card interiors. The step name is the number of 4px units, so --space-6 is 24px and --space-3-5 14px. Layout also has fluid tokens: --gutter (16 to 40px) and --section-space (48 to 96px).

Radius

One soft family that gets rounder as things get bigger: 6px checkboxes, 10px controls, 12px kanban cards, 16px board columns, 20px page panels and dialogs. No other values. The primitives are --radius-6/10/12/16/20; components use rounded-check/control/card/column/panel/dialog.

Elevation

Two-layer shadows tinted toward the warm neutral. In dark mode shadows fade and lighter surfaces carry elevation instead.

Motion

Quiet and quick, and only in answer to the person: hover in at 140ms, out at 200ms, press at 90ms. One easing for UI steps, no bounce or overshoot. Cards lift 2px on hover, buttons step up 1px and press down 1px. Reduced-motion users get instant changes.

Breakpoints

Mobile first, min-width only. Reach for intrinsic layouts and container queries before viewport breakpoints. Your current viewport is highlighted.

Also included
  • .center centers content at a max width (default 65ch).
  • .cover vertically centers one element in a min height: heroes, empty states.
  • .frame keeps media at an aspect ratio (default 16/9).
  • .prose sets measure and rhythm for long-form text.
  • .board, .editor and .list-view are the app views; see Page shells and the prototype.
  • .cq makes a wrapper a container-query scope.

Containers

.container caps content width and adds the fluid page gutter. Default is lg. .section adds vertical rhythm between page bands, and .breakout lets media bleed edge to edge inside a container.

Page shells

Shells frame a whole page; app views fill the app shell's main area.
